# The 7-beat short script

Every short follows this spine. Each beat exists to stop a specific kind of swipe. The structure is adapted from
Duncan Rogoff's public breakdown of a short that out-performed a $100 human edit.

| # | Beat | Job | Swipe it prevents | Length |
|---|------|-----|-------------------|--------|
| 1 | **HOOK** | Say what this is, ideally with a number | "What even is this?" | 1 sentence, ≤ 12 words |
| 2 | **PROOF** | Show other people already validated it | "Why should I care?" | 1 sentence |
| 3 | **HEAD FAKE** | State the common belief, then break it | "I already know this" | 1–2 sentences |
| 4 | **REHOOK** | A short line that re-opens the loop | the mid-video swipe (the biggest drop-off) | 3–7 words |
| 5 | **LIST** | 3 concrete things | "Too vague" | 3 items, ≤ 3 words each |
| 6 | **PAYOFF** | The one-line transformation + effort or price anchor | "So what?" | 1–2 sentences |
| 7 | **CTA** | One clear ask (see the rotation below) | turns attention into comments, follows, leads | 1 sentence |

Total: **75–110 words, which is 25–40 seconds.** Over ~45s, cut the head fake or a list item.

## Example (annotated)

> **[HOOK]** This free GitHub repo is a 342-hour AI course.
> *A concrete number, and "free" + "GitHub" says what it is instantly.*
>
> **[PROOF]** 56,000 people have already started.
> *Borrowed credibility before any explanation.*
>
> **[HEAD FAKE]** So you think you have to start at lesson one and grind through all 523. You don't.
> *States the belief the viewer holds, then snaps it.*
>
> Pick one goal, like build agents, and it gives you the path.
>
> **[REHOOK]** And nothing you build gets thrown away.
> *The moment someone might swipe. This line keeps them.*
>
> **[LIST]** Every lesson makes something you keep: a prompt, a skill, an agent.
>
> **[PAYOFF]** It turns your AI into a tutor. 342 hours of lessons and it costs nothing.
> *Transformation + price anchor. Repeating the hook number closes the loop.*
>
> **[CTA]** If you want the link, comment SCRATCH.

## Writing rules
- **Spoken, not written.** Contractions, short sentences, fragments. Read it out loud. If you run out of breath, split it.
- **Numbers as digits** ("342", "56,000"). Captions show them as numbers and they stand out.
- **One idea per sentence.**
- **The first sentence must be complete and make sense alone.** Nobody waits for sentence two.
- **Proof before explanation.** Show why it's worth it before you explain how.
- **Honesty:** every number comes from the source. Never round up or invent. If you can't verify it, cut it.
- **Voice:** follow `creator/profile.md`. No hype words ("insane", "game-changing", "mind-blowing").

## Rehooks are written for THIS short
A good rehook teases the next beat with a real detail. Test: if the line could be pasted into any other short, rewrite it.
- Shapes that work: "Then I timed it." · "The trick is what it switches off." · "The price is the real story." · "One line fixed it." · "Then I read the fine print."
- Banned (worn out everywhere): "Here's where it gets crazy", "But that's not even the best part", "Now watch this", "This is the part nobody talks about", "Here's the part that got me".
- Vary the head fake too. "You'd think X. It doesn't." is fine sometimes, not every time.

## Hook angles (write one variant of each)
1. **Number hook:** lead with the most surprising number. "This free tool replaced a $30/month app."
2. **Contrarian hook:** lead with the belief being broken. "Stop paying for AI courses."
3. **Result hook:** lead with the outcome. "This turns your phone into a teleprompter."

## CTA rotation: not every short asks for the same thing
The same "comment WORD" ask on every short reads like a funnel, not a person. Use it on at most 2 in 7 shorts.

| Type | Shape (fill with THIS short's specifics) | Best on |
|---|---|---|
| **Keyword** | "Comment WORD and I'll send you the [real resource]." | tutorials, free resources (only when the resource exists) |
| **Opinion** | "Would you let an AI [do the thing] unsupervised? Yes or no." | hot takes, news |
| **Experience** | "What's the one task you'd test this on first?" | tools, tutorials |
| **Pick a side** | "[A] or [B]. Which one are you using?" | comparisons, news |
| **Prediction** | "Where's this in 6 months? Call it in the comments." | news, hot takes |
| **Follow the series** | "Part 2 is the launch. Follow so you see if it breaks." | build logs, series |
| **Save / send** | "Save this for your next launch." · "Send this to the friend still paying for X." | tutorials, explainers |

Keyword rules: one short, unusual, on-topic word in CAPS (STACK, PROMPT, SCRATCH). Never a common word.
