# Scoring a breaking story

Three questions. Answer each honestly from what you actually found in the search results — don't guess.

## 1. How fresh is it?
- **Under 6 hours old** → +2
- **6–24 hours old** → +1
- **Over 24 hours (up to 48h)** → +0
- Older than 48 hours isn't "breaking" anymore — it belongs in the next `/creator-os:news` digest instead.

## 2. How many independent sources have it?
- **3 or more unrelated outlets/sources covering it** → +2 (it's actually spreading, not one blog's take)
- **2 sources** → +1
- **1 source only** → +0 (could still be real, just can't confirm size yet — say so in the report)

## 3. Does it match what this creator's audience cares about?
- **Directly matches a "watch for" keyword or a content pillar** → +2
- **In the niche but a stretch from what this audience follows** → +1
- **Technically in the niche but nobody would expect a post about it** → +0

## Total → rating
- **5–6 points → High** (🔴 post today if possible)
- **3–4 points → Medium** (🟡 worth a short this week)
- **0–2 points → skip it** (too thin, too stale, or too off-topic — don't put it in the report at all)

A story can score High on freshness and spread but still be wrong for this creator — if question 3 scores 0,
cap the total at Medium even if the math says higher. Relevance to the actual audience matters more than
raw virality.
