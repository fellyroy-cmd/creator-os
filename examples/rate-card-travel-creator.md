## Payment terms
- Deposit before production starts: 50%
- Balance due: within 7 days of the post going live
- Payment method: Wise invoice
- Currency: USD
- Revisions included: 1 round free, $50 each after
- Usage rights: organic posting only; paid-ad use is +50% for 30 days
- How long the post/link stays live: minimum 90 days

## Auto-decline
- Gambling or betting apps
- Crypto tokens or trading signals
- "Passive income" or MLM schemes
- Obvious spam or a template blast with no brand name

## Always flag me, don't draft a reply
- Any offer over $1,500
- Contracts or legal documents needing a signature
- Airlines or tourism boards tied to a destination with an active safety advisory (grey area for this niche)
- Requests for personal details, bank info or a phone call

## Disclosure
#ad in the caption and the pinned comment, plus a verbal mention in the first 15 seconds of any video segment.
